**In one paragraph.** A Canadian Representative is the person or company in Canada that a non-Canadian applicant names on its ISED certification application. Under [RSP-100, section 4.1](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/devices-and-equipment/radio-standards-procedures-rsp/rsp-100-certification-radio-apparatus-and-broadcasting-equipment), the representative is "responsible for responding to all enquiries from ISED regarding the certified product(s), including providing audit samples at no charge to ISED," and the applicant must keep the arrangement in place "for as long as the certified product is offered on the Canadian market." Your certification body will ask for a signed letter from the representative before it issues the certificate. We provide that letter and then stand behind it.
</div>

## Who needs this

Any applicant for ISED certification whose company address is not in Canada. That covers the manufacturer of record on the application, whether you are in Shenzhen, Taipei, Seoul, Tokyo, Bangalore, Munich, Cambridge or San Jose. It applies to every product certified under the Radio Standards Specifications (RSS): Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices, cellular and LPWAN modules, IoT sensors, wearables, medical and industrial equipment, automotive telematics, satellite terminals and more. It also applies when you are certifying through a foreign certification body (FCB) under a mutual recognition arrangement: the CB is recognized, but you still need a representative in Canada.

## Why a lawyer rather than a lab or consultant

The representative role is a standing legal commitment, not a one-time document. Three things follow from having a regulated law firm hold it. First, continuity: law firms are bound by professional rules on client files and successor arrangements, so the appointment does not evaporate when a consultant retires or a lab is acquired. Second, confidentiality: product details and ISED correspondence sit inside a lawyer-client relationship governed by the Law Society of Ontario. Third, independence: we do not test, certify or compete with your lab or CB, so labs and certification bodies refer clients to us without conflict. More in [why a lawyer as your Canadian Representative](/guides/why-a-lawyer-as-your-canadian-representative/).

## Frequently asked

<!--faq-->
### Is a Canadian Representative actually mandatory?

Yes, whenever the applicant's company address is outside Canada. RSP-100 section 4.1 says: "A Canadian representative is required when the applicant's company address is not within Canada." Certification bodies will not issue an ISED certificate to a foreign applicant without the representative's details and a signed letter.

### How fast can I get the attestation letter?

The same business day after payment is confirmed, Eastern Time. Card payments through Stripe clear immediately; wire transfers take one to three banking days. If you are in Asia and submit overnight, the letter is normally waiting when you start work.

### Do I need a separate letter for each product?

One letter per IC certification number. All models, HVINs and PMNs covered by that certification are included in the same letter. A new certification number needs a new letter, which is why the annual plan exists for companies with several products.

### How long does the appointment last?

Our per-certification appointment runs ten years from the date of the letter. ISED requires you to maintain a valid representative for as long as the product is sold in Canada, so if the product is still on the market at year ten, you renew. Details in [how long must a Canadian Representative be appointed](/guides/how-long-must-a-canadian-representative-be-appointed/).

### Can you revise a letter that already exists?

Yes. Added models, changed company details and corrected certification numbers are common. Use the [revision form](/revise/). Revisions within 30 days of issue are free.

### What does the representative do if ISED asks for an audit sample?

We receive the request, notify you the same day, and coordinate shipment of the sample from you to ISED. RSP-100 requires that samples be provided to ISED at no charge; the cost of the unit and shipping is the applicant's. See [ISED audit samples: who provides them and who pays](/guides/ised-audit-samples-who-provides-who-pays/).
