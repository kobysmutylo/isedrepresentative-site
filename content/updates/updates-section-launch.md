## Why a separate section

The guides on this site answer questions whose answers are meant to stay stable: what RSP-100 section 4.1 requires, [what an attestation letter must contain](/guides/attestation-letter-required-fields/), [how long the appointment lasts](/guides/how-long-must-a-canadian-representative-be-appointed/). When ISED revises a procedure or something changes in how certifications are processed, that news doesn't belong buried in a reference page — it belongs in a dated post you can check, and the affected guide gets revised at the same time.

## What we will post here

Expect updates in three categories: changes to ISED documents and procedures that bear on the Canadian Representative requirement (RSP-100 issues, related RSS and RSP documents, Certification Bureau practice); developments in the certification process that matter to foreign applicants and the labs and certification bodies that serve them; and occasional service announcements — pricing, turnaround, or scope changes to the [Canadian Representative service](/canadian-representative-service/).

Each post carries its date and, like everything on this site, is written by [Koby Smutylo](/about/), the Ontario lawyer who has held Canadian Representative appointments since 2010.

## How to follow

New posts appear on this page's parent [Updates index](/updates/) and in the site's [RSS feed](/feed.xml) alongside new guides. If a change affects an appointment you hold through us, we contact you directly — the Updates section is information for the wider certification community, not a substitute for client notices.
