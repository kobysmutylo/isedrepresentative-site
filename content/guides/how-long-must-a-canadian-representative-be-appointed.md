Under RSP-100 section 4.1, for as long as the certified product is offered on the Canadian market. That is the regulatory duration and it is open-ended. Our per-certification appointment is a ten-year contractual term that satisfies it for the typical product's life; if your product is still on sale at year ten, you renew. An annual plan covers every certification filed while it is active, each for ten years.
</div>

## Two different durations

Confusion on this point usually comes from mixing up two things.

The **regulatory duration** is set by ISED: "The applicant shall have a valid Application and Agreement for Certification Services for as long as the certified product is offered on the Canadian market" ([RSP-100 section 4.1](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/devices-and-equipment/radio-standards-procedures-rsp/rsp-100-certification-radio-apparatus-and-broadcasting-equipment)). There is no fixed number of years; the obligation lasts while the product is sold in Canada and ends when it is withdrawn.

The **contractual term** is whatever you and your representative agree. Some providers sell one-year terms with annual renewal notices. Some say "for as long as the product is sold" and bill annually. We sell a ten-year term for a single fee, because a decade covers the commercial life of nearly every wireless product and removes nine annual renewals from your compliance calendar.

Both satisfy ISED as long as the appointment is in fact maintained. The question to ask a provider is not "how long is your term" but "what happens at the end of it, and what happens if you are no longer in business before then."

## What happens at the end of ten years

Three months before expiry we contact you. If the product has been withdrawn from Canada, nothing is needed. If it is still on the market, you renew at the then-current per-certification fee and the appointment continues without any change on the ISED record, because the representative is unchanged. If you have moved to an annual plan in the meantime, the renewal is covered by it.

## What happens if the product is withdrawn early

The obligation ends when the product is no longer offered in Canada. Tell us, and we close the file. There is no refund for the unused portion of a per-certification term, which is priced as a single fixed fee, but there is also nothing further to pay.

## What happens if the representative disappears

This is the real risk in the market, and it is why duration matters. If a representative ceases to exist, is acquired, or simply stops answering, the applicant is in breach of section 4.1 the moment ISED sends an enquiry that goes unanswered. ISED can suspend or revoke a certificate for non-compliance with the procedure. Replacing the representative is straightforward if you notice in time (see [changing your Canadian Representative](/guides/changing-your-canadian-representative/)), but applicants rarely notice until the enquiry arrives.

A law firm's appointment has a structural advantage here. Law societies require lawyers to make arrangements for the continuity of client files, including on retirement, incapacity or death, and a firm's obligations to a client survive the departure of any individual lawyer. See [why a lawyer as your Canadian Representative](/guides/why-a-lawyer-as-your-canadian-representative/).

## Products with long service lives

Utility meters, industrial sensors, medical devices and automotive modules commonly stay on sale, or are re-ordered as spares, for well beyond ten years. For those products, budget for a renewal at year ten and make sure the representative you choose will still exist. We have renewed appointments first made in the early 2010s; see [case examples](/case-examples/).

## Permissive changes and new certifications

A Class I or Class II permissive change under RSP-100 section 10 that keeps the same IC number stays within the existing appointment; tell us so the letter lists the new models. A new IC certification number is a new certification and needs its own appointment, which is what the annual plan is for. See [one representative letter per IC ID](/guides/one-representative-letter-per-ic-id/).

### Is a ten-year term compliant if ISED says "as long as the product is on the market"?

Yes. ISED requires that a valid appointment exist at all times while the product is sold. A ten-year term that is renewed if the product outlives it meets that requirement, in the same way an annually renewed term does, with far fewer renewals to miss.

### Can the appointment be backdated to cover a gap?

No. If an appointment lapsed, appoint a replacement immediately and notify the CB. The gap cannot be papered over, but a prompt replacement is what ISED and the CB will want to see.

### Does the appointment transfer if I sell the product line?

No. A transfer of the certificate to a new holder is a CB process, and the new holder needs its own representative appointment. See [changing your Canadian Representative](/guides/changing-your-canadian-representative/).
