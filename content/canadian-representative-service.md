## The regulatory role we accept

RSP-100 puts three responsibilities on the Canadian Representative, and our letter accepts each of them expressly:

> "The Canadian representative shall be responsible for responding to all enquiries from ISED regarding the certified product(s), including providing audit samples at no charge to ISED. The applicant shall have a valid Application and Agreement for Certification Services for as long as the certified product is offered on the Canadian market."
> — [RSP-100, Issue 12, section 4.1](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/devices-and-equipment/radio-standards-procedures-rsp/rsp-100-certification-radio-apparatus-and-broadcasting-equipment)

In practice that means a Canadian address and a reachable Canadian contact on the certification record; a commitment to receive and answer ISED enquiries about the certified product; and a commitment to make audit samples available to ISED when requested. The representative does not make technical claims about your product and is not a substitute for the certification body or the test lab. Read [representative vs certification body vs test lab](/guides/representative-vs-certification-body-vs-test-lab/) if the division of roles is new to you.

## What is included

**The attestation letter.** A signed PDF on Smutylo Law+ letterhead, dated, identifying the applicant (legal name, address, ISED Company Number), the product (IC certification number, PMN, HVIN, model numbers), the representative (our legal name, Ottawa address, email, phone) and the acceptance of RSP-100 section 4.1 responsibilities for the stated term. We format it to the field list certification bodies actually check; see [attestation letter required fields](/guides/attestation-letter-required-fields/). If your CB has its own template, we sign that instead at no extra cost.

**ISED enquiry handling.** If ISED's Certification and Engineering Bureau or your CB contacts us about the product, we acknowledge receipt, forward the enquiry to your designated contact the same business day, and relay your response. We do not answer technical questions on your behalf without your written instructions.

**Audit sample coordination.** When ISED requests a post-certification audit sample under RSP-100 section 12.2, we coordinate shipping instructions between you and ISED so the sample arrives correctly addressed and documented. The sample itself and its shipping are at the applicant's expense, as the procedure requires.

**Record keeping.** We keep the appointment on file with your contact details and remind you to update them when they change. A stale applicant contact is the most common reason an ISED enquiry goes unanswered; see [common Canadian Representative mistakes](/guides/common-canadian-representative-mistakes/).

**Revisions.** New models added under the same certification, company name changes and address corrections are handled through the [revision form](/revise/).

## What is not included

Testing, certification filing, listing in ISED's Radio Equipment List, labelling review, or technical compliance advice. Those are the jobs of your laboratory and certification body, and we work alongside both. Canadian legal advice beyond the representative role is available separately through [Smutylo Law+](https://lawyercorporation.ca/canadian-representative/) on normal terms.

## Term and renewal

The per-certification appointment is ten years from the date of the letter. ISED's requirement is that a representative be maintained for as long as the product is offered in Canada; most wireless products are withdrawn well before year ten, but if yours is still on sale we will contact you before expiry with a renewal at the then-current per-certification fee. The annual plan covers every certification you file while the plan is active. Full treatment in [how long must a Canadian Representative be appointed](/guides/how-long-must-a-canadian-representative-be-appointed/).

### Can the certification body's own representative form be used instead of your letter?

Yes. Several CBs and FCBs use their own Canadian Representative declaration. Send it with your order and we will complete and sign it, at no additional cost.

### Do you act as representative for terminal equipment under DC-01 as well?

Yes. The same Canadian contact requirement applies to terminal equipment registered under DC-01 section 6. Tell us on the form if your product is terminal equipment rather than radio apparatus so the letter cites the right procedure.

### Will you appear as the applicant or the certificate holder?

No. You remain the applicant and certificate holder. We are the Canadian Representative identified on the application, nothing more.
