These terms govern the Canadian Representative service provided by Smutylo Law+ ("we", "us") through ISEDRepresentative.com to the applicant identified in an order ("you").

## 1. The service

On receipt of payment we will (a) issue a signed letter confirming our appointment as your Canadian Representative for the ISED certification identified in your order, for the term stated; (b) act as the Canadian point of contact for ISED and your certification body in respect of that certification, forwarding enquiries to you and relaying your responses; and (c) coordinate with you the delivery of audit samples requested by ISED. We do not test, certify, list, label or provide technical compliance advice, and we make no representation about your product's compliance.

## 2. Term

A per-certification appointment runs for ten years from the date of the letter. An annual plan covers certifications for which a letter is issued while the plan is active, each such appointment running for ten years from its letter date. You acknowledge that RSP-100 requires a Canadian Representative to be maintained for as long as the certified product is offered in Canada, and that renewal is your responsibility.

## 3. Your obligations

You will provide accurate applicant and product information; keep us informed of a current contact for the life of the appointment; respond to enquiries we forward within the time ISED or the certification body requires; supply and pay for any audit sample and its shipping; and notify us of any transfer of the certificate or change in your legal identity.

## 4. Fees

Fees are as published on the pricing page at the time of order, in US dollars, and are non-refundable once the letter has been issued. Revisions are free within 30 days of issue and subject to an administrative fee thereafter.

## 5. Termination and withdrawal

We may withdraw as representative on 60 days' written notice if you fail to respond to an ISED enquiry or audit sample request after reasonable attempts to reach you, if fees are unpaid, or if continuing would breach our professional obligations. We will notify your certification body of any withdrawal. You may replace us at any time by appointing another representative and notifying your certification body.

## 6. Liability

Our liability for any claim arising from the service is limited to the fees paid for the appointment concerned. We are not liable for delays or decisions of ISED or any certification body, or for losses arising from inaccurate information you supply.

## 7. Professional relationship

The service is provided by a law firm. The representative engagement is limited to the scope in section 1 and does not make us your general legal counsel. Information you provide is held in confidence in accordance with the rules of the Law Society of Ontario.
