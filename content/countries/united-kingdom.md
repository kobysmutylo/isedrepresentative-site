<div class="answer" markdown="1">
UK manufacturers need a Canadian Representative under RSP-100 section 4.1 like any other applicant outside Canada. The UK's mutual recognition position with Canada carried over under the Canada–UK Trade Continuity Agreement, so UK certification bodies may be recognized by ISED; check the current list. The role is distinct from the UK Responsible Person and the EU authorised representative that UK exporters already deal with.
</div>

## How certification usually runs from the UK

Testing at a UK laboratory recognized by ISED (the UK has several with long FCC and ISED experience), certification by a recognized UK or European FCB or by a North American CB. The CB requests the Canadian Representative's details and letter before issuing.

## Three different representatives

UK exporters are used to the UK Responsible Person (for UKCA-marked goods from non-UK manufacturers) and the EU authorised representative (for CE-marked goods from non-EU manufacturers). The ISED Canadian Representative is a third, unrelated role: Canadian address, ISED-specific duties, for the life of the product in Canada. None of the three substitutes for another. See [the requirement explained](/guides/canadian-representative-requirement-rsp-100/).

## What we see from UK applicants

**Scale-ups and specialist manufacturers.** UK applicants tend to be IoT, medical, defence-adjacent and industrial specialists rather than volume consumer electronics. Products have long lives and the appointment's durability matters; see [how long must a Canadian Representative be appointed](/guides/how-long-must-a-canadian-representative-be-appointed/).

**Common law familiarity.** UK clients are comfortable with a law firm holding the appointment and often ask for our terms up front; see [terms of service](/terms/).

**Entity naming.** "Limited" versus "Ltd" and "PLC" as registered at Companies House, consistently with the CB's application.

## Payment and timing

Card via Stripe in USD is immediate; wires from UK banks arrive in one to two banking days. Invoices in USD. The five-hour difference means a morning order from the UK is normally fulfilled by UK mid-afternoon.

## Audit samples

Shipped from the UK to ISED's laboratory in Canada at the applicant's cost; we coordinate. See [ISED audit samples](/guides/ised-audit-samples-who-provides-who-pays/).
