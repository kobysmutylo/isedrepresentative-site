Korean manufacturers can certify for Canada through Korean certification bodies recognized by ISED under the APEC TEL mutual recognition arrangement, but still need a Canadian Representative under RSP-100 section 4.1 because the applicant is outside Canada. Korea's profile of large product families, module makers and automotive tier-one suppliers shapes how letters are structured.
</div>

## How certification usually runs from Korea

Testing is done at recognized Korean labs and certification is issued either by a Korean FCB recognized by ISED or by a North American CB, depending on the manufacturer's global certification programme. Either way the CB requests the Canadian Representative's details and letter before issuing. Confirm the CB's recognition on ISED's current list.

## What we see from Korean applicants

**Large families, many HVINs.** Korean consumer-electronics and appliance makers certify wide model families. One letter covers every model under an IC number; ask for a reissue when models are added by permissive change. See [one representative letter per IC ID](/guides/one-representative-letter-per-ic-id/).

**Module makers.** Korean Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and cellular module manufacturers supply integrators worldwide. The module maker needs the representative for each module certification; integrators do not. Module makers with several products benefit from the [annual plan](/pricing/). See [RF modules](/industries/rf-modules/).

**Automotive tier-ones.** Korean telematics and infotainment suppliers certify in their own names for vehicle programmes that last a decade. The supplier, not the automaker, needs the representative, and the appointment must last the programme. See [automotive telematics](/industries/automotive-telematics/).

**Group structures.** Where a Korean group certifies through an overseas subsidiary (often in the US or Vietnam), the applicant entity is the one that needs the representative. Tell us which entity will be on the application.

## Payment and timing

Card via Stripe in USD is immediate; wires from Korean banks arrive in one to two banking days. Orders placed during the Korean working day are handled overnight Korea time and the letter is normally waiting the next morning. We correspond in English; Korean-language enquiries are welcome and we reply in English.

## Audit samples

Shipped from Korea to ISED's laboratory in Canada at the applicant's cost; we coordinate. See [ISED audit samples](/guides/ised-audit-samples-who-provides-who-pays/).
