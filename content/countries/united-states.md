US companies are the group most surprised by the requirement. The FCC has no equivalent, and the fact that a US TCB can issue the ISED certificate under the Canada–US mutual recognition arrangement leads many to assume nothing further is needed. RSP-100 section 4.1 looks at the applicant's address: a US applicant needs a Canadian Representative. We share your business hours and issue the letter the same day.

## How certification usually runs from the US

Most US TCBs are recognized by ISED as foreign certification bodies under the Canada–US MRA and issue FCC grants and ISED certificates from the same test campaign. The TCB asks for the ISED Company Number and the Canadian Representative letter as part of the ISED application. This is usually the moment a US regulatory manager first hears of the requirement. See [from FCC to ISED](/guides/fcc-to-ised/).

## What we see from US applicants

**"Our TCB handles Canada."** It handles the certification; it is not your representative. The representative must have a Canadian address. See [the requirement explained](/guides/canadian-representative-requirement-rsp-100/).

**"Our Canadian distributor can sign."** It can, if it has a real Canadian place of business and accepts in writing the duty to respond to ISED and provide audit samples for as long as the product is sold in Canada. In our experience distributors often decline, and an appointment tied to a distribution agreement tends to lapse when that agreement ends. See [representative vs certification body vs test lab](/guides/representative-vs-certification-body-vs-test-lab/).

**"We have a Canadian subsidiary."** If the subsidiary is the applicant on the ISED application and has a genuine place of business, no separate representative is needed. If the US parent is the applicant, it is.

**Startups and non-RF companies.** Hardware startups and companies whose product happens to contain a radio (beverage dispensers, fitness equipment, appliances, medical devices) hit the requirement late. Bevi, a Boston maker of connected beverage dispensers, is an example; see [clients](/clients/).

**Procurement.** US corporate procurement often requires a vendor form and W-8BEN-E for a foreign supplier; we provide both. Purchase orders are accepted for annual plans.

## Payment and timing

Card via Stripe, immediate. ACH and wire accepted. We are in Ottawa, Eastern Time, so an order placed in the morning from the East Coast or before early afternoon from the West Coast is normally fulfilled the same day.

## Audit samples

Shipped from the US to ISED's laboratory in Canada at the applicant's cost, documented as a regulatory sample. We coordinate and can advise on the customs entry. See [ISED audit samples](/guides/ised-audit-samples-who-provides-who-pays/).
