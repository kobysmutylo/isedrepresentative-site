Japanese manufacturers can certify for Canada through Japanese certification bodies recognized by ISED under the APEC TEL mutual recognition arrangement, or through North American CBs, and in either case need a Canadian Representative under RSP-100 section 4.1. Japanese clients typically want complete documentation up front, a formal invoice, and a representative that will still be in place for the full, often long, life of the product.
</div>

## How certification usually runs from Japan

Testing at recognized Japanese labs; certification by a recognized Japanese FCB or by the North American CB the manufacturer uses for FCC work. The CB requests the Canadian Representative details and letter. Confirm the CB's recognition status on ISED's current list.

## What we see from Japanese applicants

**Documentation first.** Japanese regulatory teams usually ask for our terms, a sample letter and an invoice before ordering. All are available: [terms of service](/terms/), the letter structure in [attestation letter required fields](/guides/attestation-letter-required-fields/), and a pro-forma invoice on request.

**Long lifecycles.** Industrial equipment, medical devices, automotive components and imaging products from Japan stay on the market for many years. RSP-100 requires the representative for as long as the product is sold in Canada, and Japanese clients are attentive to the question of what happens at the end of a term. See [how long must a Canadian Representative be appointed](/guides/how-long-must-a-canadian-representative-be-appointed/) and [why a lawyer as your Canadian Representative](/guides/why-a-lawyer-as-your-canadian-representative/).

**Multiple certifications per programme.** Products with several radios, or families certified separately by band, mean several letters. The [annual plan](/pricing/) suits manufacturers with a steady certification flow.

**Entity naming.** Use the English legal name on the corporate registration ("Kabushiki Kaisha" rendered as "Co., Ltd." or "Corporation" consistently with your CB's application).

## Payment and timing

Card via Stripe in USD is immediate; wires from Japanese banks arrive in one to two banking days. A formal invoice in USD is provided. Orders placed during the Japanese working day are handled overnight Japan time and the letter is normally in your inbox the next morning. We correspond in English.

## Audit samples

Shipped from Japan to ISED's laboratory in Canada at the applicant's cost; we coordinate. See [ISED audit samples](/guides/ised-audit-samples-who-provides-who-pays/).
