<div class="answer" markdown="1">
German and other EU manufacturers can have their ISED certificate issued by a European certification body recognized by ISED under the Canada–EU mutual recognition arrangement, but still need a Canadian Representative under RSP-100 section 4.1. The role is not the same as the EU authorised representative under the Radio Equipment Directive: it is narrower, ISED-specific, and lasts for the life of the product in Canada.
</div>

## How certification usually runs from Germany

German manufacturers typically test at a German or European laboratory recognized by ISED and have the certificate issued either by a European FCB recognized under the Canada–EU MRA or by a North American CB. The CB asks for the Canadian Representative's details and letter. Confirm the CB's recognition on ISED's current list.

## Canadian Representative versus EU authorised representative

The RED's authorised representative acts for a non-EU manufacturer in respect of EU conformity obligations. The ISED Canadian Representative acts for a non-Canadian applicant in respect of ISED enquiries and audit samples for a specific certification. A German manufacturer selling into Canada does not need an EU authorised representative for that; it needs the Canadian one. The two roles have different scopes and should not be confused in the technical file. See [the requirement explained](/guides/canadian-representative-requirement-rsp-100/).

## What we see from German and EU applicants

**Industrial and automotive applicants.** German applicants skew to industrial automation, metering, automotive tier-one suppliers and medical devices: products with long service lives and formal change control. The appointment must last as long as the product is sold in Canada; see [how long must a Canadian Representative be appointed](/guides/how-long-must-a-canadian-representative-be-appointed/). Kamstrup, a Danish metering manufacturer, is an example of the category; see [clients](/clients/).

**Entity naming.** Use the legal form as registered (GmbH, AG, SE, GmbH & Co. KG) consistently with the CB's application.

**Data protection.** Our handling of personal data is described in the [privacy policy](/privacy/); we hold only the contact details needed to operate the appointment, handled under Canada's PIPEDA; our payment and form processors are named in the policy.

**Procurement.** Supplier onboarding forms and a pro-forma invoice are provided on request. Purchase orders accepted for annual plans.

## Payment and timing

Card via Stripe in USD is immediate; SEPA is not available for USD invoices, but wires from EU banks arrive in one to two banking days. Invoices in USD; the six-hour difference means an order placed in the German morning is processed early in the Ottawa day and the letter is normally issued the same afternoon German time.

## Audit samples

Shipped from the EU to ISED's laboratory in Canada at the applicant's cost; we coordinate. See [ISED audit samples](/guides/ised-audit-samples-who-provides-who-pays/).
