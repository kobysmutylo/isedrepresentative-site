If you have received an attestation letter naming Smutylo Law+ (ISEDRepresentative.com) as an applicant's Canadian Representative and want to confirm it is genuine and still in force, send the IC certification number or applicant name and the letter date to [info@isedrepresentative.com](mailto:info@isedrepresentative.com), call +1 613 869 5440, or use the form below. ## What we confirm

That the letter was issued by Smutylo Law+; the applicant, IC certification number (or company number and product, where the IC number was pending) and models it covers; the issue date and any reissue dates; and that the appointment is current. We do not disclose the applicant's contact details, test reports or anything beyond what the letter itself states.

## Who may ask

Certification bodies and foreign certification bodies reviewing an application, ISED's Certification and Engineering Bureau, test laboratories preparing a submission, and Canadian importers or distributors doing diligence on a supplier. Applicants can ask for a fresh copy of their own letter through the [revision form](/revise/).

## How to check without contacting us

Every letter carries our legal name, the Ottawa address that appears on the [contact page](/contact/), and the signatory's name. Koby Smutylo is a licensee of the Law Society of Ontario (44441E), which can be confirmed on the [Law Society's public directory](https://lso.ca/public-resources/finding-a-lawyer-or-paralegal/lawyer-and-paralegal-directory). A letter that gives a different address, a different signatory, or a QR code in place of a signature did not come from us.

### Is a QR code or blockchain verification required on a Canadian Representative letter?

No. RSP-100 section 4.1 requires that the representative's details be identified on the Application and Agreement for Certification Services; it prescribes no form, seal or verification device for the letter. Some providers add a QR code. A certification body that wants to verify our letter can do so by email, phone or this page.

### How quickly do you confirm?

Within one business day, Eastern Time. ISED and certification body enquiries are acknowledged the same business day.

### Can you confirm a letter issued years ago?

Yes. We keep every letter issued since 2010 on file with its reissue history.
