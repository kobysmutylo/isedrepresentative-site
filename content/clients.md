## Roku

Streaming players, sticks and Roku TV reference platforms with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth radios, certified in volume and across model families. The work is characteristic of a consumer-electronics client: frequent new certifications, many HVINs under each, and tight launch schedules where the representative letter must not be the item holding up the CB.

## Kamstrup

A Danish manufacturer of smart electricity, water and heat meters with integrated radio modules. Utility metering illustrates why the appointment's duration matters: meters stay in the field for fifteen years or more, so the representative named on the certificate has to be there for the long run.

## Bevi (Hydration Labs, Inc.)

A Boston-based maker of connected beverage dispensers with Wi-Fi and cellular connectivity. A typical case of a US company that is not a traditional RF manufacturer but ships a radio-containing product into Canada and discovers the section 4.1 requirement at the certification stage.

## Eurofins

Eurofins' electrical and electronic testing laboratories certify products for manufacturers worldwide and route foreign clients' Canadian Representative needs to us. Laboratories value a representative that is independent, answers the same day, and will still hold the appointment when the lab's project is long closed. See [for labs and certification bodies](/for-labs-and-certification-bodies/).
