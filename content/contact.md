## ISED and certification body enquiries

If you are writing from ISED's Certification and Engineering Bureau or from a certification body about a product for which Smutylo Law+ is the Canadian Representative on record, email info@isedrepresentative.com with the IC certification number in the subject line. We acknowledge the same business day.
